A week later, we took her to [Center of Hope](https://centerofhope.ro/), an excellent veterinary hospital for rescued animals. It's where we take every animal we rescue.

The eye couldn't be saved, but the pain could be taken away. The vet closed it for good, neatly and gently.

**Every pirate gets their patch somehow. Cara got hers from a very kind surgeon.**
