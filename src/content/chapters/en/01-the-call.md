It started with a phone call.

Some kind people had found a young, caramel-coloured dog on the street. Someone had hurt her, and she had lost her left eye. **We won't tell that part of the story. She doesn't dwell on it, so neither will we.**

They didn't know what to do next. But they'd found our number on an old adoption ad for another dog we once rescued, so they called to ask for advice.

One thing led to another. (With dogs, it usually does.)
